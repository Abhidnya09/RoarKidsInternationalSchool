📋 Parent Information

School Policies & Guidelines

Everything parents need to know to partner with us in your child's journey at Roar Kids.

General Information for Parents

✦
Parents are expected to attend all events and programmes organized by the school to motivate their ward.
✦
In case of leave, inform the class teacher and report in the diary with parent's signature.
✦
For long absences due to sickness, produce an authentic medical certificate upon return.
✦
Please inform the school about specific health problems, allergies, and recent illnesses.
✦
Parents/Guardians must check their ward's lessons daily and guide them to follow school rules.
✦
No student will be allowed to leave school before closing time except in emergencies.
✦
Every student must participate in school games and activities unless medically unfit.
✦
Students with contagious or infectious diseases will not be permitted to attend school.

Attendance & Punctuality

✦
We encourage 100% attendance. Absence must be supported by a medical certificate.
✦
Students must be punctual for school, CCA, and all school events.
✦
Late-comers: first and second instances receive a warning. A third instance requires parents to take the student home.
✦
Absence without valid reason will be deemed as truancy.

Parent–Teacher Meetings

✦
Parents can meet teachers on the 1st and 3rd Saturday between 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM.
✦
Parents shall not meet teachers during working hours.
✦
Batch I (Morning): Last Saturday of every month at 10:30 AM
✦
Batch II (Afternoon): Last Saturday of every month at 3:30 PM
✦
PTM attendance is compulsory for all parents for better understanding of your child's progress.

Birthday Celebrations in School

✦
Parents are requested not to send gifts, cake, or expensive items for birthday celebrations.
✦
Parents may send candies or small bars of chocolate for distribution in class.
✦
Donating a Reading Book to the school library to mark the occasion will be appreciated.
✦
Parents are requested not to send any gifts for teachers.

Personal Grooming Guidelines

✦
Students should wear a neat and clean uniform every day.
✦
In the rainy season, all children should wear black sandals.
✦
For PG & Nursery — no diapers are allowed to be worn in school.
✦
Girls with long hair should tie it in 2 plaits with black ribbons. Hair must be neatly tied.
✦
All boys must keep their hair short. Exceptions are made on religious grounds.
✦
Sleeveless dresses are not allowed on any occasion.
✦
For Playgroup, Nursery & Sr. KG — an extra pair of dress should be sent in the school bag.

Food Policy

✦
The school strictly follows Vegetarianism within the premises. No non-vegetarian items allowed.
✦
Avoid sending junk food in tiffin boxes.
✦
Naming should be done on napkins, tiffin box, and water bottle.
✦
Every child must bring 2 towel napkins (1 big and 1 small) daily for the tiffin box and general use.
